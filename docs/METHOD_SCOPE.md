# Numerical and interpretive scope

## Supported statements

- At fixed composition, geometry, and DFT+U settings, more than one locally
  self-consistent Co 3d occupation matrix can be reached.
- Complete spin-up and spin-down 5 x 5 matrices are the primary state
  descriptor. Diagonal occupation vectors are discovery labels only.
- A Ni-Kr target matrix transfers into a distinct operational target family
  in the other tested metal/gas environments.
- Matching the guest-free calculation within that target family changes the
  net adsorption energy much less than the raw loaded-cell energy shift.
- Two control definitions place the Pd/Pt selectivity response in the
  approximately 9-14 meV range.
- The Co3-squarate calculations preserve two distinct matrix solutions in
  selected settings, while the single-site adsorption pilot remains below
  the local resolution boundary.

## Claims that are not supported

- The selected states are global thermodynamic ground states.
- The Pd/Pt states are exact cross-metal identity of the common Ni-Kr 5 x 5
  matrix.
- The 0.6 and 0.12 diagonal thresholds are universal convergence or identity
  thresholds.
- The result establishes a transferable MOF descriptor.
- Co3-squarate supplies a second material ranking or a full adsorption-site
  search.
- Bader populations, the 2 Angstrom guest-sphere integral, and absolute
  differential-density integrals are interchangeable quantities.
- A converged SCF energy by itself proves that the same electronic solution
  was retained.

## Reporting rule

For a state-sensitive adsorption comparison:

1. record the selected electronic solution;
2. match the guest-loaded and guest-free cells within a stated operational
   target;
3. compare complete spin-resolved matrices before subtracting energies;
4. requalify the endpoint at production cutoff and k-point settings;
5. report the local numerical range and any unresolved near-degeneracy.

This is a minimum reporting sequence, not a claim that all possible SCF
solutions have been enumerated.
